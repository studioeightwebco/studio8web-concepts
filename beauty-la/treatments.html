Does it hurt?

The needle is very tiny, so it feels more like a pinprick than a regular shot.

What happens after?

You may have a small amount of bruising and swelling. Ice packs help, and after a few days you should notice the results.

What else can Botox treat?

Injections in the underarms, hands, face and feet can decrease sweating. Injections around the head and neck can help prevent chronic migraines.

Sculptra

Sculptra stimulates your natural collagen production to firm and smooth the cheek region and tighten skin along the jawline. It was the first injectable collagen stimulator, made with poly-L-lactic acid (PLLA-SCA).

  • Typically 3 sessions, spaced at least 3 to 4 weeks apart
  • Clinical study participants saw results as soon as 1 month, lasting up to 2 years

What should I do after treatment?

Massage the treated area to evenly distribute Sculptra. Follow the 5-5-5 rule: 5 minutes, 5 times a day, for 5 days in a row.

What is collagen?

Collagen is a protein that helps maintain your skin's shape and structure. By age 40, most of us have lost up to 20% of our natural collagen.

Microneedling & PRP

Microneedling builds collagen and elastin to help with fine lines, enlarged pores, uneven complexion and acne scarring. Pair it with your own platelet-rich plasma (PRP) for a vampire facial.

How do I prepare?

Follow the guidelines for 24 to 48 hours before your appointment. Stop retinoids and acid-based products. On the day, arrive with a freshly washed face and no lotion, perfume or serums.

Does it hurt?

Microneedling is non-surgical and minimally invasive. Any sensation is superficial and temporary.

When will I see results?

Most people notice changes within 24 to 72 hours. Actual results show in two to four weeks, as your skin produces collagen. Results on atrophic acne scars can be permanent.

Who is a good candidate?

Most people, if your skin is not prone to easy scarring and you have no active skin conditions or infections. It helps with skin laxity, fine lines, enlarged pores, blemishes, sun damage, hyperpigmentation, acne scarring, dull skin and stretch marks.

What do PRP under-eye injections do?

PRP contains growth factors that stimulate collagen, helping tighten skin around the eyes and lessen fine lines like crow's feet.

PRP for Hair Loss

PRP hair restoration uses platelet-rich plasma from your own blood, injected into the scalp to stimulate hair growth. It is a minimally invasive option for thinning hair in both men and women.

  • A simple blood draw
  • Platelets are separated in a centrifuge
  • PRP is injected into the scalp, often with microneedling
  • Multiple sessions are often needed for best results

What happens after?

Redness, headaches and slight pain are normal and go away quickly. Avoid direct sun for 24 hours and take care when washing your hair.

Chemical VI Peel

VI Peel treats pigmentation, acne, scars and fine lines in one in-office visit. Its medium-depth formulations make the most impact with the least amount (if any) of downtime.

How does a VI Peel work?

The peel lifts and exfoliates skin cells so your body produces new, healthier and less pigmented cells. Exfoliation also leaves the surface softer and smoother.

Laser Hair Removal

InMode Diolaze treatments use a diode laser that heats the root of the hair to prevent future growth. A built-in cooling system makes the process virtually painless. Gentle on all skin types, for legs, arms, underarms, upper lip, chin and more.

Book Now

How do I prepare?

Avoid sunlight and let the hair grow. Your technician will trim it to the right length for the laser. On the day you will wear protective goggles, and a cooling gel may be applied.

How do I care for my skin after?

Avoid direct sun on the area for several weeks. Use strong sunscreen once it is safe to apply lotions. Avoid any other hair removal on the treated area.
