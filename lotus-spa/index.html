Frequently asked questions

Is there a free consultation before any treatment?

Yes. After booking your treatment and arriving for your appointment, a professional team member does a full free consultation before starting any treatment. You’ll also create a plan with your final goal and the number of sessions needed to achieve it.

When should I book a package rather than a single session?

Every treatment can be purchased as a single session or as a discounted package. Depending on your goal, you might be happy with a single treatment. For more dramatic results you’ll need several sessions, and buying a package saves you a lot compared with paying for each treatment separately.

Which body sculpting procedure is most effective for fat loss?

Cryoskin therapy is the most dramatic fat loss treatment we offer. It can be positioned anywhere on the body, and it’s fast, easy, painless and fully customizable.

Do you have any current promotions?

We always have discounts on packages. We also take $50–$100 off your procedure price when you refer a client who books any treatment. Call or email us with any questions about this offer.
