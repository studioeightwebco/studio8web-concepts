What does a cosmetic nurse do?

Our cosmetic nurses perform chemical peels, CoolSculpting®, facial injections, dermal fillers, facials, microneedling and Sculptra. Beyond treatments, they:

Offer insights and advice

Develop tailored skin care routines and recommend lifestyle or diet adjustments to support complete skin health.

Stay current

Always search for new, more effective ways to strengthen skin and treat various conditions.

Care with the future in mind

Address existing concerns while promoting long-term skin health, so your skin stays strong and radiant for longer.

Non-invasive solutions

Injections, dermal fillers, laser treatments, chemical peels and more, with real advantages.

  • Minimal discomfort: much more comfortable than surgery
  • Faster recovery: no incisions, and you can typically resume daily activities almost immediately
  • Versatility: from fine lines and wrinkles to acne scars and pigmentation
  • Natural-looking results: we aim to enhance your skin’s natural appearance

Commonly asked questions

Can I get personalized skin care advice from your team?

Absolutely. Our team in Burbank prides itself on offering personalized skin care advice to each patient. We develop tailored skin care routines and may suggest lifestyle or dietary changes to support overall skin health.

What makes your cosmetic services stand out?

Our commitment to personalized care, affordability, and the use of advanced medical technology. Our experienced doctors, nurses, and aestheticians create customized treatment plans for the unique needs of each patient.

How do your cosmetic nurses stay updated?

Our cosmetic professionals are committed to continuous learning and integrate new techniques and technologies into our practice, so we provide the most effective treatments available.
