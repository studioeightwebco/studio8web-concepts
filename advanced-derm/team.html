Meet our team

Our highly skilled team provides a wide range of medical and cosmetic treatments to help our patients have healthier and more beautiful skin.

Amir Larian, MD

Board-certified dermatologist

Dr. Amir Larian received his medical degree at UC San Diego School of Medicine. A diplomat of the American Board of Dermatology and a Fellow of the American Academy of Dermatology, Dr. Larian offers complete Medical, Surgical and Cosmetic Dermatology Services.

Book with Dr. Larian

Lisa Benest, MD

Medical and cosmetic dermatology

Dr. Lisa Benest received her medical degree at UC Irvine. She specializes in Medical and Cosmetic Dermatology, as well as Skin Cancer Surgery. Dr. Benest is known for her holistic and nutritional approach to medicine, as well as her friendly and personal care.

Book with Dr. Benest

Ron Birnbaum, MD

Board-certified dermatologist

Dr. Ron Birnbaum is a board-certified dermatologist and a Fellow of the American Academy of Dermatology. His focus is on the humane treatment of common and uncommon skin diseases in adults and children.

Book with Dr. Birnbaum

Nikki Tang, MD

Mohs surgeon

Dr. Tang is a diplomate of the American Board of Dermatology and fellow of the American College of Mohs Surgery. She is dedicated to helping her patients achieve their best skin and hair. As a dermatologist with specialty training in skin cancer surgery and facial reconstruction, she has a nuanced understanding of facial anatomy and proportions as well as advanced procedural skills.

Book with Dr. Tang

Julie Alexander, PA

Physician assistant

Julie Alexander is a board-certified Physician Assistant with over 4 years of experience in medical dermatology. I’m passionate about providing evidence-based, empathetic care and enjoy working closely with patients to develop treatment plans tailored to their unique needs.

As Valedictorian of Southern California University of Health Sciences, I’m also committed to mentoring the next generation of healthcare providers as a clinical preceptor. I look forward to meeting you and supporting you on your journey to healthier skin!

Book with Julie Alexander, PA

Adriana Regalado, BSN, RN

Registered nurse

Adriana comes to us with experience as an R.N. with specialized training in cosmetic treatments. She has a bachelor's degree in Biomedical Science as well as her BSN from the University of Texas at Arlington. Adriana has a passion for working with her patients to create and maintain the results they want!

Book with Adriana Regalado, RN

Yvonne Ruiz

Aesthetician

Yvonne specializes in treating acne, blackheads, post-inflammation hyperpigmentation, and overall skin wellness. She has been in the field of dermatology for 16 years making her a sought-after professional for those with challenging skin concerns.

Book with Yvonne Ruiz
