Your first visit

Knowing what will happen at your appointment can make it easier to relax and focus on your goals.

  1. Check in

    Our front desk team helps you complete any remaining intake paperwork and confirm your medical history, medications and skincare products.

  2. Meet your dermatologist

    A dermatologist or clinician reviews your concerns in detail, examines your skin in good lighting, and discusses your short- and long-term goals.

  3. Your plan

    We outline treatment options, how each works, what it will feel like and how much downtime to plan for.

  4. Aftercare and follow-up

    Before you leave, we review aftercare, suggest products that support your results, and schedule any recommended follow-up visits.

When should you see a dermatologist?

Schedule a visit for the following and more.

  • Persistent acneIf you have struggled with acne and over-the-counter treatments do not work, we may recommend stronger prescription medications or explore chemical peels or laser therapy options.
  • Suspicious molesMonitor new or existing moles and report any changes. We can perform an exam and recommend a biopsy if we suspect a mole to be cancerous.
  • Eczema, psoriasis or rosaceaThese chronic skin conditions cause discomfort and often affect self-esteem. Our team can help you manage symptoms through strategic care.
  • Hair lossIf your hair is excessively shedding or thinning, we can identify the underlying cause and perform appropriate treatments.
  • Skin infectionsOur dermatologists routinely treat bacterial, viral, or fungal skin infections, and can advise you on preventing future infections.
  • Skin cancer screeningsCrucial for early detection. If you have a family history of skin cancer or spend a lot of time in the sun, reach out for routine screenings.
  • Spider veinsOur dermatologists may be able to remove visible spider veins through laser treatment or sclerotherapy.

Prioritize your skin health

Your skin is the largest organ in your body and plays a crucial role in protecting you. Steps you can take:

  • Use sunscreen and avoid prolonged sun exposure
  • Maintain a healthy diet
  • Drink water daily so your skin stays hydrated
  • Clean your skin routinely with gentle cleansers
  • Practice stress-management techniques
  • Get quality sleep each night
  • Avoid smoking, as it accelerates aging and damages skin

Frequently asked questions

What services does a medical spa offer?

Medical spas combine traditional spa services with advanced medical procedures. At Advanced Dermatology Center, we offer treatments such as chemical peels, laser therapies, microneedling, and injectables like Botox. These services are designed to improve skin health, enhance appearance, and address specific skin conditions.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

It depends on your specific skin needs and goals. During your initial consultation at our Burbank location, our professionals will assess your skin and discuss potential treatments, so you fully understand your options and choose the plan that aligns best with your desired outcomes.

Are medical spa treatments safe?

Safety is paramount at Advanced Dermatology Center. All treatments are carried out by trained professionals under strict adherence to medical guidelines, using state-of-the-art equipment and techniques, and all procedures meet California's rigorous medical standards.

How should I prepare for my first visit?

Gather your relevant medical history and skincare concerns. We recommend avoiding heavy makeup and products on your appointment day to allow for an accurate assessment of your skin. If you have more questions, feel free to contact us beforehand.

What are the benefits of choosing a local med spa in Burbank?

Accessible follow-up appointments and continuity of care. Being nearby makes it easier to adjust your skincare plan as your needs evolve, and our understanding of the local California climate helps us personalize your care.
