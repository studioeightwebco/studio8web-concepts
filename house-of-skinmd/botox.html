Your BOTOX® treatment is expertly administered by a licensed professional in a quick, minimally invasive procedure. Most clients experience only mild discomfort. You’ll leave feeling rejuvenated and more confident.

To maintain optimal results, we recommend treatments every 3 to 4 months. Regular treatments help prevent deeper lines from forming.
