Hyaluronic acid, a natural substance

Hyaluronic acid, or HA, is a natural substance in the body that delivers volume to the skin. Modified HA is the main ingredient behind JUVÉDERM®.

Four fillers, four areas

Ideal Aesthetics offers four JUVÉDERM® fillers, each designed for different areas of the face.

Lips & smile lines

Juvederm Ultra XC

Helps add volume to different areas of the face without surgery. Great for adding volume to lips and smile lines.

$700 per syringe
Lips & lipstick lines

Volbella XC

For lip augmentation and correction of perioral "lipstick lines" in adults over 21. A subtle enhancement of lips for a more hydrated appearance.

$800 per syringe
Wrinkles & folds

Vollure XC

For moderate to severe facial wrinkles and folds, including the "parentheses lines" around the nose and mouth, for a smoother appearance in adults over 21.

$850 per syringe
