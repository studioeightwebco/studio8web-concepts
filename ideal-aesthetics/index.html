Meet Kim Ngo PA-C

Kim is a board certified plastic surgery and aesthetic physician assistant and has been in the cosmetic industry for over 14 years.

She completed her undergraduate studies in Biological Sciences with an emphasis on Neurology, Physiology, and Behavior at the University of California at Davis, and obtained her Physician Assistant degree at Charles Drew University of Medicine and Science.

She strives to subtly enhance natural beauty with individualized treatments based on each person's unique needs.
