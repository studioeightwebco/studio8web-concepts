About Skin Specifics

Founded in 2003 by Anusha Dahanayake, NP, MSN, with a second location opened in 2006.

Anusha Dahanayake, NP, MSN

Founder and Clinical Director

A nationally board certified nurse practitioner with 15 years’ experience in anti-aging aesthetics and three decades in medicine. A graduate of UCLA and UC Irvine, she worked in emergency medicine for 18 years at Los Angeles hospitals. Update years of experience

Anusha specializes in the non-surgical BEAUTIPHICATION method of assessment developed by Montreal surgeon Arthur Swift, MD, designing a precise, individual plan for each patient that uses injection treatments to restore symmetry and balance. She trains with dermatologists, plastic surgeons and injection specialists in California, Europe and Asia, and created a line of custom-formulated skin care available at both offices.

“The foundation of my practice is helping my patients reach their aesthetic goals while facilitating safe, healthy and graceful aging. It’s really about treating the whole person.”

Dr. Richard Hahn, MD

Medical Director

A general and vascular surgeon with 50 years in medicine and a 1972 graduate of the USC Keck School of Medicine. Dr. Hahn also practices aesthetic medicine, including neurotoxins, dermal fillers and lasers, and introduced Anusha to aesthetic medicine in 2001.
