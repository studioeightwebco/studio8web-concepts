Wrinkle relaxers

Botox

A simple, non-surgical treatment that temporarily smooths moderate to severe facial wrinkles. It takes about 10 minutes and a few small injections, and results can last up to 4 months.

Used for: Frown lines between the brows, forehead lines, crow’s feet, a gum-revealing smile, excessive sweating.

Dysport

A prescribed injectable that relaxes facial muscles to treat moderate to severe wrinkles. It only affects the targeted area, so surrounding muscles move normally for a soft, natural look.

Used for: Moderate to severe wrinkles.

Jaw Reduction

Botox can reduce an enlarged masseter muscle for a softer, more oval jawline, with very little pain and almost zero downtime.

Used for: Square or enlarged jaw, masseter reduction.

Dermal fillers

Juvéderm

Our preferred facial filler for its smooth consistency and longer-lasting results. This hyaluronic acid gel restores volume, smooths folds like smile lines and adds volume to lips. Results are instant and can last up to a year.

Used for: Smile lines, lips, under-eyes, fine lines above the lip.

Add starting priceBook Now

Restylane & Restylane Lyft

FDA-approved hyaluronic acid fillers we use to add volume and soften wrinkles and folds, and most of all to create or recreate balance and proportion in a face.

Used for: Volume, folds, nose and chin reshaping.

Radiesse

A calcium-based filler that corrects moderate to severe wrinkles and stimulates new collagen. We use it to add volume to the cheeks and deep nasolabial folds, with results lasting up to one year.

Used for: Flat cheeks, deep nasolabial folds, chin, temples.

Add starting priceBook Now

Belotero Balance

A newer generation hyaluronic acid filler made for very superficial fine lines, without the bluish discoloration other fillers can cause near the surface.

Used for: Etched lines, upper lip, lower nasolabial folds, superficial tear troughs, crow’s feet.

Sculptra

Poly-L-lactic acid that restores volume gradually and increases your own collagen around the injection site, for soft contours instead of an overfilled look. Maintained with touch-up treatments.

Used for: Sunken areas, folds, overall facial volume.
