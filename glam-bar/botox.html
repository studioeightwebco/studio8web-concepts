Treatment areas

Beyond wrinkle reduction

Lips

Lip Flip

A small amount of product around the upper lip relaxes the muscle so the lip rolls outward subtly, for a slightly fuller-looking upper lip without the volume of dermal filler.

Smile

Gummy Smile

Relaxes the specific muscles that make the upper lip rise too far when smiling. For appropriate candidates, this creates a more balanced smile while keeping natural expression.

Chin

Chin Dimpling

A dimpled or "pebbled" chin can come from increased mentalis muscle activity. Carefully placed Botox relaxes this muscle for a smoother-looking chin, customized to keep natural facial movement.

Neck

Neck Bands

Vertical neck bands can become more noticeable with age and repeated muscle activity. Botox may temporarily relax the platysma muscles, for a smoother-looking neck and a more refined transition from jawline to neck.

Jaw

Jaw Slimming & Masseter Reduction

For clients with prominent masseter muscles, a nonsurgical approach to a slimmer-looking lower face. Temporarily reducing masseter activity may gradually soften a broad or square jaw for a more tapered profile, without surgery.
