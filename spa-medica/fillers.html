FAQs

Before your treatment

Who is this for?

You're an excellent filler candidate if you have fine lines and wrinkles. Dermal fillers plump the skin, fill in wrinkles and smooth the skin.

What can I expect immediately after treatment?

You'll see immediate results on the injected area that will continue to improve for one week after treatment.

How long do results last?

The filler's longevity depends on the viscosity of the filler and the area treated.

Is this treatment safe when pregnant?

No, dermal fillers are not recommended when pregnant or breastfeeding.
