FAQs

Before your treatment

Who is this for?

An excellent candidate is an adult with healthy skin, moderate elasticity and wrinkles in areas near the eyes. These injections improve the appearance of dynamic wrinkles.

What can I expect immediately after treatment?

Within 24–48 hours most patients notice fine lines and wrinkles fading. Best results begin to show within 3 to 7 days, with full effects within 2 weeks.

How long do results last?

Results from Botox, Dysport or Xeomin are not permanent. Effects usually last about 3–4 months.

Is this treatment safe when pregnant?

No, wrinkle reducers are not recommended when pregnant or breastfeeding.
