Spa policies

Your first visit

Please arrive 15 minutes before your first appointment to check in and complete intake paperwork.

Deposit and cancellation

A non-refundable deposit is required when booking. Cancellations, changes or no-shows within 72 hours forfeit the deposit. With 72 or more hours' notice the deposit can move to another appointment. An invoice is sent when you book, and your time is held for 24 hours until the deposit is received. Confirm one cancellation window: the current site also says 24 hours and a $50 fee

Appointment reminders

We send a text or email reminder 24 hours before your appointment. If a reminder doesn't arrive, the cancellation policy still applies.

Late arrival

If you arrive late we'll do our best to give you the full treatment, but it may be shortened to keep other guests on time. Shortened treatments are charged at full value.

Payment and gratuity

We do not bill insurance. Prices may change without notice. We accept cash, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Care Credit and Skinlastiq gift cards, but not checks. Gratuity is optional. Financing is available through Cherry.

Refunds

Services and packages are non-refundable. Products are non-refundable but may be returned for store credit within 7 days with a documented allergic reaction; defective products can be exchanged within 7 days. Prescription products cannot be returned or exchanged.

Provider requests

We'll try to book you with your preferred provider, but illness, vacations and schedule changes may prevent it.
