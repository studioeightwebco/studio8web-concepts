Our facial therapies

Top requested service

Enzyme Peel Therapy

Hydrolyze your skin cells and clear out the dead skin buildup in a single session while stimulating new collagen and elastin. This makes Enzyme Peel Therapy great for combating acne and premature aging.

add price

Gold Patch Therapy

A luxurious facial that uses a mask of pure 24K gold to activate skin cells to rejuvenate, hydrate, tighten and detox the skin. Especially beneficial for dull, tired skin looking to reduce fine lines and wrinkles.

add price

Oxygen Therapy

Helps brighten, hydrate and rejuvenate the skin by delivering highly concentrated oxygen molecules to the epidermis. Well-suited for parched skin looking for deep hydration, and for sensitized skin post laser treatments or plastic surgery, as it soothes the skin and reduces swelling.

add price

Lifting Therapy

Uses Gold Jewelry Treatment to help improve circulation and soften fine lines and wrinkles. This treatment can help those suffering from dark circles and eye bags.

add price

Lymphatic Detox Massage

Also known as the Deep Tissue Massage, this facial helps diminish the appearance of wrinkles, brightens your skin by removing toxins, and loosens up any tight muscles to help you feel more relaxed.

add price

Vitamin C Therapy

Well-known for its brightening benefits. It uses Electro-Ion and electrophoresis to ionize the Vitamin C to help unveil your brightest and most radiant skin, and is also known to help reduce the appearance of freckles and pigmentation.

add price

Aqua Peel Therapy

A safe, non-invasive treatment to rejuvenate and deeply cleanse the skin. Through vacuum extraction, pores are unclogged of impurities, blackheads, and whiteheads, so your skin can be refreshed.

add price
