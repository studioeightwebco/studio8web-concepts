Laser Q & A

From the Laser Clinic page. confirm which laser these answers cover

What about the pain during the procedure?

Usually anesthetic ointment is not necessary, but it may vary depending on the individual and the treatment area. You may feel a slight tingling.

What is the procedure time?

It takes about 10 to 20 minutes based on the whole face.

What is the procedure effect?

It may be accompanied by swelling or erythema shortly after the procedure, but this disappears after a few days. After 1 to 2 weeks, the pigment of the treated area will gradually fade.

What are the precautions after the procedure?

After the procedure, there is no problem in everyday life. However, please continue to use sunblock as usual.

What is the number of procedures?

You can get satisfactory results in a single session, but you will get more satisfactory results if you are treated consistently every two weeks.
