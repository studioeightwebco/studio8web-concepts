meet sarah

Sarah Fillmore started out packing coffee bags at a Chicago specialty roastery in 2014. She learned to taste, brew, evaluate and roast, and became a licensed Q Arabica Grader along the way.

She came home to California and opened Good Intentions with her partner, Tristan, to share her love of coffee with the community.
