About Us

Our mission is to create a welcoming environment where people are appreciated and kindness is kindled, so our staff and community can realize their full potential.

We hold one core belief: all people are of value and should always be treated as such.

Our values

Welcoming & Accepting

A space where everyone is welcomed and accepted for who they are.

People Are the Bottom Line

Each person in our company, community, suppliers and throughout the coffee supply chain is respected and valued over dollars and cents.

Positive & Kind

Feeding those we serve with a positive and kind demeanor.

Sarah's Story

Our founder, Sarah Fillmore, earned a bachelor's degree in sociology from San Francisco State University, then moved from the Bay Area to Chicago.

In January 2014 she took a job packing coffee bags at a local specialty roastery. From there she learned to taste, brew, evaluate and roast coffee, and went on to work in roasteries, cafés and green coffee sales. That work took her all across the US.

She returned to California and founded Good Intentions Coffee with her life partner, Tristan, to share her love of coffee with the community.
